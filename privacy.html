Privacy Policy

ArkAide collects nothing. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts.

Effective October 12, 2026. ArkAide is made by Court Bowman ("we"). This policy explains what the app keeps and where.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't collect, receive, or have access to any information about you or your children. The app has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code that sends data anywhere.

What the app keeps, and where

So the games can work the way your family sets them up, the app keeps:

  • children's names (optional; a child can be added with no name), card pictures and colors, skill levels, and stars;
  • grown-ups' names (what the kids call them, like "Grandma");
  • voice recordings that grown-ups choose to make;
  • settings, like the narrator and sound volume.

These are stored on your iPad and in your own private iCloud (Apple's CloudKit), so they can reach your other iPads. Only people signed in to your Apple ID, and anyone you invite with Invite family…, can see them. We can't. iCloud storage is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

ArkAide is made for young children. We don't collect personal information from children, and children can't record anything: the microphone is used only in the grown-ups area, behind a parental gate (a math question), when a grown-up taps record. Everything in the grown-ups area, including links and email, is behind that gate.

The microphone

The app asks for the microphone only so grown-ups can record their own voices for the games. Recordings stay on your iPad and in your private iCloud.

Deleting your information

In the grown-ups area, Delete all data erases everything from your iPad and your iCloud. Deleting the app removes what's on the iPad. What's in iCloud can also be removed in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
